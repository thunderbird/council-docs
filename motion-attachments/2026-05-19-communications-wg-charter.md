# Purpose

There is a recognized need to increase awareness and engagement regarding the Thunderbird Council's activities. To ensure a healthy democratic process, we must improve how we communicate the Council's impact and the importance of participation.
The primary objective of this Work Group is to work with MZLA to design a comprehensive communication plan that encourages a broader and more diverse pool of community members to participate as both candidates and voters in upcoming elections.

# Scope

- **Strategy Development:** Define the key messaging and channels required to reach potential candidates and electors.
- **Collaboration with MZLA:** Work closely with MZLA’s Community Team to ensure the plan aligns with Thunderbird’s brand voice and effectively resonates with the global audience.
- **Content Definition:** Determine what specific information regarding Council activities and election processes needs to be highlighted to drive engagement.
- Council Presence: Find new interactive ways for the Council to be contacted and to be an active presence in the Community.
- **Proposal for Approval:** Consolidate the strategy into a formal Communication Plan to be presented to the Council for a final vote.

# Goals & Deliverables

- **Draft Communication Plan:** A document outlining the timeline, platforms (e.g., thunderbird.net, social media, community forums), and content types for election promotion.
- **Candidate Recruitment Kit:** Clear information and assets explaining the benefits and responsibilities of being a Council member.
- **Voter Engagement Strategy:** A plan to increase the number of people joining the election roll and actively participating in the voting process.
- **Council Approval:** Successfully present the plan to the Council and obtain formal approval via vote.

# Risks

- **Information Overload:** If the communication is too dense or technical, we risk losing the interest of the community audience.
- **Alignment:** Failure to coordinate effectively with MZLA teams could lead to inconsistent messaging or delays in deployment.
