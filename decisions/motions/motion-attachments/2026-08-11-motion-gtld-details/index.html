    2026 08 11 motion gtld details

    The Council kindly asks MZLA to investigate whether Thunderbird might apply for a new generic top-level domain through ICANN for use with Thundermail, Thunderbird Pro, and other current or future Thunderbird services.

    The Council believes that a Thunderbird-controlled gTLD could be a major strategic opportunity for the project. It could give Thunderbird a distinctive public identity, strengthen Thundermail as a service, create new privacy-preserving email options for users, provide new ways for the community to participate in and support the project, and potentially create a long-term source of sustainable revenue aligned with Thunderbird’s mission.

    To ensure a broad perspective, the Council encourages MZLA to look beyond .mail or .bird. While those are strong examples, it would be highly valuable if MZLA could consider all suitable and available gTLDs that could serve Thunderbird users and the wider community.

    The Council invites MZLA to focus the investigation on the potential benefits and practical paths forward, exploring aspects such as:

    1. A stronger Thunderbird identity

    Whether a Thunderbird-controlled gTLD could create a memorable, mission-aligned identity for Thunderbird services, such as addresses in the style of @thunder. or other names that clearly connect users to Thunderbird.

    1. A better Thundermail user experience

    How a dedicated gTLD could improve Thundermail onboarding, account identity, branding, trust, and long-term product differentiation from other email providers.

    1. Privacy-preserving email namespaces

    The possibility of offering users personal namespaces, supporter namespaces, or subscriber namespaces that allow them to create separate addresses or aliases for different purposes. These could provide many of the privacy benefits of temporary aliases while appearing more like ordinary email addresses than traditional tagged/subaddressed aliases.

    1. Community and contributor value

    How the gTLD could be used to recognize and support Thunderbird community members, contributors, add-on developers, localizers, donors, supporters, working groups, events, or community initiatives through meaningful Thunderbird-related email addresses or namespaces.

    1. Open standards and user empowerment

    How a Thunderbird-controlled gTLD could support Thunderbird’s values around open standards, user choice, privacy, portability, decentralization, DNSSEC/DANE, and user-controlled communication.

    1. Sustainable funding opportunities

    Whether some namespaces or domains could be offered as part of Thunderbird Pro, supporter benefits, organizational plans, community programs, or public registration in a way that supports Thunderbird’s long-term financial sustainability while remaining consistent with the project’s values.

    As a conclusion to this investigation, the Council would welcome a written recommendation identifying the most promising gTLD candidates, the strongest potential use cases, the likely product and community benefits, and whether MZLA would suggest applying in the current ICANN round, preparing for a future round, partnering with an existing registry or registry service provider, or pursuing a different domain strategy.

    The Council would deeply appreciate it if MZLA could provide this recommendation within 14 days of this motion passing, or sooner if possible, to preserve the option of applying in the current ICANN application round (closes on August 12, 2026).

    This motion is intended to suggest to do an investigation, product exploration, and recommendation only. It does not by itself require MZLA to submit a gTLD application, pay ICANN application fees, enter into a registry agreement, or launch any particular domain product without further Council interaction.
